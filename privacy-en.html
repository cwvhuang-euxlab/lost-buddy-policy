Lost Buddy Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 26, 2026

Welcome to Lost Buddy (hereinafter referred to as “Lost Buddy,” “the Service,” or “we”). We value your personal privacy and protect your personal data in accordance with the Personal Data Protection Act of the Republic of China (Taiwan) and other applicable laws and regulations. This Privacy Policy (hereinafter referred to as the “Policy”) explains how we collect, use, store, disclose, and protect your personal data.

When you begin using the Service, whether by registering and signing in, browsing without signing in, submitting a case, or using other features, you acknowledge that you have read and understood the methods of collection, processing, and use of personal data described in this Policy.

I. Types of Personal Data We Collect

When you use the Service, we may collect the following data based on your use of the Service and the content you voluntarily provide:

  1. Account and Identification Information

    When you sign in to the App through a third-party account such as Apple or Google, we will collect the unique identifier (UID), email address, and nickname authorized by the relevant third-party platform.

  2. User-Generated Content (UGC)

    Information regarding lost-dog and sighting cases that you voluntarily publish or upload through the Service, including pet photos, descriptions of characteristics, last known location, time, contact phone number, email address, and other relevant information that you voluntarily provide.

    If you submit a case through the website without signing in, we may also collect the email address you provide so that we may contact the person who published the case when necessary.

  3. Precise Location Information

    To provide the core “lost-dog search map” feature, when you allow the App or website to access your device's location permission, we may obtain your precise GPS coordinates to assist you in confirming your current location and selecting a case location.

    Your current location is used only as a reference for map positioning and selecting a case location. The location will only be submitted and stored as part of the case information after you manually select and confirm the case location.

  4. Device and Technical Information

    The App may collect your device model, operating system version, unique device identifier, IP address, and service crash records.

    The current Policy does not separately list website-side device, browser, IP address, or analytics data unless such data is actually collected by the Service.

II. Purposes of Use of Personal Data

The data we collect will be used within the necessary scope of the following specific purposes:

  1. To provide, maintain, and optimize the Service's lost-dog search map, case publishing, and sighting-report matching features.
  2. To authenticate users, maintain account security, and provide account management services, including account deletion.
  3. To process user reports and prevent inappropriate conduct, fraud, and maliciously false reports.
  4. To conduct necessary technical data analysis to improve the performance and user experience of the Service.
  5. When necessary, to contact the person who published a case through the contact information provided by the user in order to handle matters related to the case.
  6. To process and use relevant data as required by law or as necessary to maintain the security of the Service, protect users' rights and interests, and prevent unlawful conduct.

III. Disclosure Regarding Third-Party Service Providers and Data Storage

The Service uses cloud platform technologies provided by Google Firebase, including but not limited to Firestore database and Cloud Storage, as the core data storage and backend environment.

  1. Firebase Data Storage

    Case information, photos, and personal data related to cases that you publish will be transmitted and stored through the cloud services provided by Google Firebase using relevant technical mechanisms. We will take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data.

  2. Provision of Personal Data to Third Parties

    Except as required by laws and regulations, for investigation by judicial authorities, or as necessary to perform these Terms of Service, we will not sell, rent, or arbitrarily share your personal data with unrelated third-party organizations.

    Service providers such as Google Firebase that provide the necessary technical infrastructure for the Service may process relevant data to the extent necessary to provide their services.

  3. Public Display of Case Information

    Case information that you voluntarily submit may, depending on the content you select or provide when publishing the case, be publicly displayed on the Lost Buddy lost-dog search map and related Service interfaces for viewing by other users.

    Publicly displayed content may include pet photos, descriptions of characteristics, case dates and times, locations, and contact information you provide.

    Because the purpose of case information is to assist in the recovery of lost dogs, when publishing a case, you should be aware that information you voluntarily provide may be viewed, retained, or shared by other users. Before submitting information, please ensure that the content you provide is suitable for public disclosure.

IV. User Rights and Data Retention

Under the Personal Data Protection Act, you have the right to request access to, review, obtain copies of, supplement, correct, stop the collection, processing, or use of, and delete your personal data.

  1. Account and Data Deletion

    Registered users may directly request “Delete Account” at any time through the settings feature within the App.

  2. Specific Processing Period

    After you formally submit an account deletion request, we will, within 7 business days, completely remove or irreversibly anonymize your account credentials, personally identifiable data, and non-public personal profile stored in the cloud database (Firebase).

  3. Retention of Historical Sighting Records

    To maintain the integrity of historical public lost-dog search records, lost-dog or sighting case content that you previously published on the map will, after you delete your account, be retained in an anonymized form displayed as “Lost Buddy User,” in order to protect the rights and interests of other members of the community.

  4. Data Rights of Non-Registered Users

    Non-registered users who wish to correct, update, or delete a case or personal data they have submitted may submit a request through the methods provided by the Service. Before processing such a request, we may take reasonable steps to verify the requester's identity or authority to manage the relevant case.

  5. Retention and Public Display of Case Data

    Case data submitted by users will, in principle, be retained until the user takes the case down or the case is closed.

    A case is considered closed when its status is updated to “Found” or another status indicating that the case has ended. After a case is closed, no longer publicly displayed, or is otherwise no longer shown on the lost-dog search map, the relevant data may still be retained in the Service's database and backend systems and stored and processed in accordance with the purposes and methods described in this Policy.

    The removal of a case from public display does not mean that the relevant data has been deleted from the Service's database or backend systems.

V. Period, Region, Parties, and Methods of Use of Personal Data

  1. Period of Use

    Unless otherwise provided by law or agreed otherwise, personal data will be used for the duration necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected and to complete related business activities. Case data will, in principle, be retained until the user takes the case down or the case is closed. After a case is no longer publicly displayed, the relevant data may still be retained in accordance with this Policy.

  2. Region of Use

    Your personal data may be processed or stored within the Republic of China (Taiwan) or in overseas cloud service environments as necessary to provide the Service.

  3. Parties Using the Data

    The parties that may use personal data include the Service operator, technical service providers necessary to provide the Service, and government agencies, judicial authorities, or other authorities or persons legally entitled to request access to such data.

    Case information that you voluntarily submit and designate as public may be viewed by other users of the Service.

  4. Methods of Use

    Personal data may be collected, processed, and used electronically, through automated means, or by other lawful methods, including for account management, case publishing, map display, case-related contact, data storage, Service operation and maintenance, and information security.

VI. Choice Regarding the Provision of Personal Data

You may decide whether to provide the personal data requested by the Service.

However, if you refuse to provide data necessary for a particular feature, you may be unable to use that feature. For example, if contact information is required as part of the case publishing process and you refuse to provide it, you may be unable to complete the case submission.

For data that is not necessary, you may decide whether to provide it.

VII. Amendments to the Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to amend this Policy at any time in response to changes in laws and regulations, feature updates, or information security requirements. Any amended content shall become effective upon publication on this page, and the “Last Updated” date at the top of the page will be updated accordingly.

If you continue to use the Service after the Policy has been amended, you will be deemed to have read and understood the amended Policy.
